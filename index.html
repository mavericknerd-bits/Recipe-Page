Creamy Garlic Pasta

25 mins Easy 4 Servings

This creamy garlic pasta is rich, flavorful, and perfect for a quick family dinner. Made with fresh garlic, parmesan cheese, and a creamy sauce, it's guaranteed to impress.

Ingredients

  • 300g pasta
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • ½ cup grated parmesan cheese
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta according to package instructions.
  2. Melt butter in a pan and sauté garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add cream and simmer for 3-4 minutes.
  4. Stir in parmesan cheese until smooth.
  5. Add cooked pasta and mix well.
  6. Season with salt and pepper.
  7. Garnish with parsley and serve hot.
Chef's Tip: Add a splash of pasta water to make the sauce extra silky and help it coat the noodles perfectly.
